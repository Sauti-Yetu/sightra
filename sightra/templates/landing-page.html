What Sightra helps you do

Sightra is an AI-assisted visual perception and information-access product. It is built for everyday independence — not as a generic camera app, and not as a replacement for your own judgement.

  • Understand your surroundings

    On-demand scene and obstacle analysis with spoken descriptions where supported. Requires camera permission and a network connection.

  • Access printed information

    Read text, documents, signs, currency and supported uploaded images through spoken output and a transcript.

  • Interact accessibly

    Use supported voice commands together with TalkBack or VoiceOver, and save theme, text size, contrast and spoken-language preferences.

How Sightra works

Vision and Reader run when you ask them to. They need a signed-in session, a camera or an uploaded image, and an internet connection for analysis.

  1. 1Choose Vision to understand a scene, or Reader to listen to printed text.
  2. 2Capture a camera frame, or upload a supported photo of a document, sign or note.
  3. 3Allow analysis to run. This can take a few seconds and needs the network.
  4. 4Hear spoken guidance and, where available, review an on-screen result or transcript.
  5. 5Retry or choose another action if processing fails or the result is unclear.

Who Sightra is for

Sightra is for people who are blind or have low vision, and for anyone who benefits from spoken access to visual information. Caregivers and accessibility stakeholders can also evaluate it as assistive technology.

It is a support for awareness and access. It does not replace your judgement, mobility skills, white cane, guide dog or other devices you already trust.

Available today

  • On-demand Vision scans with spoken scene guidance
  • Reader for live text, documents, signs, currency and uploaded photos
  • Supported voice commands and spoken-language preferences
  • Theme, contrast, larger text and other accessibility controls
  • Installable home-screen app (PWA) and a signed-in personal workspace

In development

Sauti Yetu Assistive Venture

Sauti Yetu Assistive Venture builds practical, human-centred assistive technology. Sightra is our flagship product: an evolving way for people who are blind or have low vision to access visual information with greater independence, confidence and dignity.

We use AI as a tool for perception and spoken access — not as a substitute for people. Feedback and testing with people with visual impairments guide how Sightra develops. Our longer-term direction is a more accessible world in which assistive technology helps people navigate information and environments with greater autonomy.

Trust and safety

Sightra provides AI-assisted information to support awareness and access to visual information. Results may be incomplete or incorrect. Sightra is not a substitute for a white cane, guide dog, mobility training or other appropriate safety measures. Always use your judgement and established mobility aids.

Scene descriptions are informational. They are not guaranteed obstacle detection or verified navigation instructions.
