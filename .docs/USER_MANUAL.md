# Sightra — User Manual

This guide explains how to use Sightra safely and effectively. Sightra is developed by **Sauti-Yetu** to support blind and visually impaired users—it does **not** replace a white cane, guide dog, or human assistant.

---

## 1. What Sightra does

- Uses your device **camera** to look at your surroundings
- Detects common objects (people, vehicles, furniture, and more)
- Speaks **short audio guidance** about hazards and your environment
- Lets you ask optional **text questions** about the scene (where supported in the app)

---

## 2. Before you start

### 2.1 What you need

- A smartphone or computer with a **modern browser** (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox)
- **Internet connection** (required for sign-in and cloud analysis)
- **Camera permission** when using Live Vision
- Optional: headphones for privacy in public spaces

### 2.2 Create an account

1. Open the Sightra website.
2. Choose **Sign up** and enter email and password, or use **Continue with Google**.
3. After sign-in, open the **Dashboard**.

### 2.3 Install as an app (optional PWA)

When your browser offers **Install Sightra** or **Add to Home Screen**, accept to launch Sightra like an app. Offline mode only shows a limited offline page—you need internet for live vision.

---

## 3. Using Live Vision

1. From the Dashboard, open **Vision** (or Live Vision).
2. Allow **camera access** when prompted.
3. Point the camera in the direction you are walking or need to understand.
4. Wait for spoken or displayed feedback after each analysis cycle.

### 3.1 Understanding alerts

- **Urgent messages** (e.g. stop, vehicle, wait) mean possible immediate danger—**stop and verify** with your cane, dog, or hearing before moving.
- **Caution messages** suggest obstacles or people nearby—slow down and explore carefully.
- **General descriptions** help orient you when no urgent hazard is detected.

### 3.2 Tips for best results

- Hold the device steady briefly while analysis runs.
- Use in **well-lit** areas when possible; very dark scenes reduce accuracy.
- In rain or glare, treat alerts as **less reliable**.
- Do not walk while focusing only on the phone—keep one hand free for your mobility aid.

## 4. Other features (overview)

| Feature | Purpose |

| **Voice assistant** | Voice-oriented help UI (capabilities depend on deployment) |

---

## 5. Accessibility options

- Use the **accessibility panel** (when shown) to adjust contrast or text size if available on your screen.
- Screen reader users: sign-in and dashboard pages use standard web forms; report barriers to support.

---

## 6. Privacy

- Video is sent to the server for analysis during Live Vision sessions.
- Do not point the camera at private documents or others’ faces without consent.
- Account data is handled under your organization’s privacy policy—contact Sauti-Yetu for details.

---

## 7. Safety disclaimer

**Sightra can be wrong.** Objects may be missed or mislabeled. Spoken advice may be delayed. Always:

- Use your **primary mobility and orientation skills first**
- Stop at curbs, steps, and traffic even if Sightra is silent
- Seek human help in unfamiliar or high-risk areas

---

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---------|----------|
| Cannot sign in | Check email/password; try Google sign-in; check internet |
| Camera blocked | Browser settings → allow camera for this site |
| No speech | Turn up volume; check silent mode; retry analysis |
| Slow responses | Move to stronger Wi‑Fi; wait a few seconds between frames |
| App offline | Reconnect to internet; refresh the page |

---

## 9. Getting help

- Contact your **Sauti-Yetu support channel** or file an issue at the project repository (if provided to you).
- For emergencies, use local emergency services—not Sightra.
